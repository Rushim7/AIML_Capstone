Query 5 - Highest rated books with categories
======================================================================
SELECT
                b.title,
                c.category_name,
                b.rating,
                b.price_gbp,
                b.in_stock
            FROM books b
            JOIN categories c
                ON b.category_id = c.category_id
            ORDER BY b.rating DESC,
                     b.title ASC
            LIMIT 10
